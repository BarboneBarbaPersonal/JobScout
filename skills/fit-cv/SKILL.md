# Fit my CV to a job posting

## Input
A message with three parts, as the JobScout board sends them:
- `JOB:` the role, company, location and link;
- `--- JOB AD ---` the full job description (if it says the ad text was not available, open
  the link and read the ad there);
- `--- MY CV ---` Andrea's master CV. This is the **only** source of facts about him.

## The one rule: facts only
Use only facts that are in the CV: roles, employers, dates, numbers, tools, languages,
achievements. You may reword, reorder, shorten and choose emphasis. You may never add
experience, numbers, tools, certifications or responsibilities that are not in the CV, and
never inflate a number. If the job asks for something the CV does not show, write it as a
visible gap: `[GAP: <what the ad asks for> - add only if true]`.

## Steps
1. **Read the ad.** List the 5-8 requirements that matter most (skills, experience, domain,
   languages, location, seniority) and the exact keywords the ad uses.
2. **Fit check.** For each requirement: covered (quote the CV evidence), partly, or missing.
   Give a fit score from 1 to 10 with one line of reasoning.
3. **Tailor the CV** (same structure as the master):
   - Headline line under the name: the target role, in the ad's words where true.
   - Professional summary: 3-4 sentences aimed at this role, using the ad's keywords only
     where the CV supports them.
   - Work experience: keep every role and its dates; reorder and reword the bullets so the
     most relevant ones come first; bring the ad's keywords into bullets that truly match.
     Cut or shorten bullets that do not help for this role (keep at least one per role).
   - Skills and languages: put the ones the ad asks for first.
   - Insert `[GAP: ...]` markers where a requirement is missing, at the place it would go.
4. **Create a Google Doc** in Andrea's Drive (Google Drive connector), titled
   `<Company> - <Role> - CV`, with these sections in order:
   1. *Fit summary*: score 1-10, top 3 strengths with CV evidence, gaps.
   2. *Tailored CV*: the full tailored CV, ready to copy into his CV template.
   3. *What changed and why*: 4-8 short lines (e.g. "Moved the Dynatrace adoption bullet
      first: the ad's main requirement is platform adoption").
   4. *Gaps to check*: the `[GAP: ...]` items in one list, and any keyword left out
      because the CV does not support it.
5. **Reply** with the Doc link, the fit score and the gaps, in 5 lines or fewer.

## Before you finish (check)
- Every number and employer in the tailored CV appears in the master CV.
- No tool, certification or responsibility was added.
- All gaps are marked, not filled.
- If something is uncertain, mark it as a gap rather than guessing.
